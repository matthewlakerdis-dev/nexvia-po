Nexvia Quote to PO Lines — v1.55

INSTALL / UPDATE
1. Extract this ZIP and open nexvia-quote-to-po-v1.55.user.js.
2. In Tampermonkey, open the existing Nexvia - Quote to PO Lines script.
3. Replace all its contents with the new script, save, then refresh Nexvia.
4. Keep only one copy enabled. Read the PDF again to replace the old preview.

FIXES
- Reads Capral-style order acknowledgements with stacked Quantity / Ordered,
  Estimated / Del. Date and Value / Line Total headings.
- Quantity comes from Quantity Ordered; Value with $/EA is the unit price.
- The item sequence and estimated date do not become quantity or product text.
- Product codes, dimensions and wrapped finish text stay in the description.
- Item totals must reconcile to the AUD subtotal, and subtotal plus GST must
  match Invoice Total. Ambiguous or inconsistent values stop the read.
- Includes v1.54 area-based pricing and all earlier fixes.
